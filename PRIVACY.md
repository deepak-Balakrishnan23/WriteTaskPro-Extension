# Privacy Policy

WriteTask Pro processes writing assistance and reminder data locally in the browser.

## What data is handled
- Text you choose to rewrite, summarize, or check for grammar
- Reminder/task items you create in the extension
- Basic theme preference saved for the UI

## How data is used
- Writing text is processed locally to provide rewrite, summary, and grammar features
- Tasks and settings are stored in `chrome.storage.local` so they remain available on your device
- The extension does not send your text, tasks, or settings to any external server

## What is not collected
- No account data
- No browsing history collection beyond the active page needed to run the requested feature
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising identifiers
- No sale or transfer of personal data

## Permissions
- `storage`: saves tasks and theme settings locally
- `alarms`: schedules local reminder notifications
- `contextMenus`: provides right-click actions you request
- `tabs` and `activeTab`: opens the sidebar on the active page and updates reminders on open tabs
- `scripting`: injects the content script on the active tab when needed after install/reload
